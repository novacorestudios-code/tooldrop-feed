Privacy Policy — ToolDrop: Power Tool Deals

Package ID: com.tooldrop.deals · Last updated: November 19, 2026

ToolDrop ("the App") helps workshop owners, contractors, and DIYers browse US power-tool deals and calculate battery kit value ($/Ah). We built ToolDrop to work with no account, no sign-up, and minimal data handling.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

ToolDrop does not require registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or home address. Your selected battery brand filters, saved deal bookmarks, onboarding preference, and feed cache tags stay stored locally on your device using Android Room and DataStore Preferences.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob + UMP)

ToolDrop displays ads served by Google AdMob (including banner, native, and frequency-capped interstitial ads). Google AdMob and its mediation partners may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP-based approximate location, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Before requesting ads where required by law (such as EEA, UK, or US state privacy regulations), ToolDrop uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and honor your consent choice. You can review or change your consent preferences at any time inside the app under Alerts & Saved → Brands → Ad Privacy Settings when applicable.

3. Optional Notifications

If you choose to subscribe to brand deal topics and grant notification permission on Android 13+, ToolDrop may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) topic subscriptions scoped only to generic tool brands (not personal identity).

4. Third-Party Retailer Links

Tapping Get Deal opens external retailer websites (such as Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon, Acme Tools, Tool Nut, Harbor Freight, Woot, Northern Tool, CPO Outlets, Ace Hardware, or Direct Tools) in your device's web browser. Any browsing or purchases on third-party sites are governed by that retailer's privacy policy. Prices and availability shown in ToolDrop reflect public deal feeds at the time of capture and may change at checkout.

5. Children's Privacy

ToolDrop is designed for general adult/DIY audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Trademark Notice

ToolDrop is an independent deal aggregator and calculator and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by DeWalt, Milwaukee, Makita, Ryobi, Bosch, Craftsman, Ridgid, Harbor Freight, or any listed retailer.
